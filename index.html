How to set up UHF Player on Apple TV 4K: tips 2026

✍️ Chloe Webb · 📅 October 2026 · ⏱️ 3 min read · 🛡️ Verified Review

UHF Player is a popular IPTV app for Apple TV 4K. This guide shows you how to install it, enter your subscription details, and tweak settings for smooth playback in 2026.

You only need an Apple TV 4K and an active IPTV subscription.

What you need

  • Apple TV 4K (tvOS 15 or later)
  • UHF Player app (download from the App Store)
  • An IPTV subscription that provides an M3U URL or Xtream Codes login
  • Optional: Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1) and a buffer setting of 5,000 ms to reduce buffering

Step‑by‑step setup

  1. On your Apple TV remote, press the Home button. Open the App Store.
  2. Search for “UHF Player” and select Get to install the app.
  3. Once installed, open UHF Player from the home screen.
  4. On the welcome screen, tap Add Playlist.
  5. Choose either M3U URL or Xtream Codes, depending on what your provider gave you.
  6. Enter the server URL and your login credentials exactly as provided. Double‑check for typos.
  7. Go to Settings > Playback inside UHF Player and set the buffer size to 5,000 ms. This helps prevent freezing.
  8. For extra stability, change your Apple TV’s DNS to Cloudflare: Settings > Network > Select your Wi‑Fi network > Configure DNS > Manual and enter 1.1.1.1.

For more details on optimizing Apple TV network settings, check out How‑To Geek.

If a step fails

  • Make sure your Apple TV is connected to the internet. Run a speed test if needed.
  • Verify that your IPTV subscription is active. (UHF Player does not come with built‑in channels; you need an outside subscription.)
  • Re‑enter the M3U URL or Xtream Codes – a single wrong character can block the connection.
  • Restart your Apple TV: Settings > System > Restart.
  • If the app crashes, uninstall and reinstall UHF Player from the App Store.

FAQ

Does UHF Player work on Apple TV 4K?

Yes. UHF Player is available for tvOS and runs on all Apple TV 4K models with tvOS 15 or newer.

Do I need a subscription to use UHF Player?

Yes. UHF Player is a media player, not a content provider. You must add your own IPTV subscription URL or Xtream Codes from a provider.

How can I fix buffering in UHF Player?

Increase the buffer size to 5,000 ms inside the app’s playback settings. Also set your Apple TV’s DNS to Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) to reduce ISP throttling.

What DNS is best for IPTV on Apple TV?

Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) or Google (8.8.8.8) are widely recommended. Use the manual DNS configuration in your Apple TV network settings.

Can I use any IPTV subscription with UHF Player?

Most providers that give you an M3U URL or Xtream Codes will work. Check with your provider if they support UHF Player. For example, services like SubTV IP (subtvip.store) support many players including TiviMate and IBO Player Pro.

How do I update UHF Player on Apple TV?

Updates are managed through the App Store. Go to App Store > Updates and install any available update for UHF Player.

Why are channels not loading in UHF Player after setup?

First verify that your subscription is active. Then check that you entered the correct URL and credentials. If the problem continues, restart your Apple TV and ensure your internet connection is stable.
